**Description (4,000)**
```
AnyPortal is a reader driven by website rules. Paste the URL of a site's category list page, and the server analyses the page structure and generates a reading rule. From then on that site sits on your shelf like a source: lists, title pages, chapters and the reading view are all rendered natively by the app.

Four kinds of content are supported: novels, comics, video and audiobooks.

How it works
• In Rule Catalog → My Generations, paste the URL of a category list page (a paginated page that lists titles), pick the content type and submit.
• Generation usually finishes within minutes and notifies you by push. When it fails, the app tells you why, and retries are offered where they make sense.
• Discover lists example sites you can add with one tap.
• Favorites, reading progress and history stay on your device; with iCloud sync on they sync between your own devices, where we cannot access them.

Coins
• New accounts receive 3,000 coins; each rewarded ad adds 20; coins can also be bought in the app.
• A normal generation costs 100 coins and a hard one 300; zero-cost failures are refunded.

Content and privacy
• Content from the sites you add is fetched by your device directly from those sites, never through our server.
• Accounts use Sign in with Apple only; we do not ask for or store your name or email address.
• Privacy policy: https://anyportal.online/privacy
• Support: https://anyportal.online/support

AnyPortal provides no content and is not responsible for the sites you add; please follow each site's terms of use.
```
